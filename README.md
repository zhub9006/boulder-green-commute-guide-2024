# Boulder Green Commute Guide

A comprehensive guide to sustainable commuting in Boulder, Colorado -- walking and biking routes, travel times, and tips for making eco-friendly travel choices.

## Featured Commute Analysis

**Route: 45 Oak Avenue to 1800 Broadway, Boulder, CO**

This guide provides a detailed analysis of walking and biking options between these popular Boulder locations.

### Route Details

Both routes follow the same core path through Boulder's grid:

1. Oak Avenue -- Start at 45 Oak Avenue, head south
2. 19th Street -- Continue south on 19th Street (longest segment: ~2.2 km)
3. 20th Street -- Brief jog east on 20th Street
4. Pearl Street -- Historic Pearl Street Mall area
5. 18th Street -- Continue south on 18th Street
6. Canyon Boulevard -- Final segment heading west toward Broadway

### Walking Route

- **Total Distance:** 4.03 km (2.5 miles)
- **Typical Time:** 50-55 minutes at a moderate pace
- **Difficulty:** Easy to Moderate

**Key Tips:**
- Boulder's elevation (5,430 ft) means great cardio workout -- hydrate well!
- Route passes through Pearl Street Mall area, great for a coffee stop
- Well-maintained sidewalks throughout most of the route
- Winter: route still plowed, dress in layers

### Biking Route

- **Total Distance:** 4.03 km (2.5 miles)
- **Typical Time:** 10-15 minutes
- **Difficulty:** Easy

**Key Tips:**
- 19th Street is a major north-south corridor with bike lanes
- Boulder has 200+ miles of bike routes city-wide
- Consider Boulder BCycle bike-share stations along the route
- Year-round bike commuting is feasible -- Boulder maintains bike lanes in winter

## Why Green Commuting Matters in Boulder

- **Altitude Advantage:** At 5,430 feet, every trip counts for air quality
- **Mountain Community:** Boulder's unique ecosystem is sensitive to vehicle emissions
- **Health Benefits:** Active commuting combats effects of high altitude
- **Community Impact:** Boulder ranks among top U.S. cities for sustainability
