# Walking Route: 45 Oak Avenue to 1800 Broadway

## Route Summary

- **Distance:** 4.03 km (2.5 miles)
- **Elevation Gain:** ~150 ft (net descent)
- **Typical Duration:** 50-55 minutes
- **Active Pace Duration:** ~30 minutes
- **Difficulty:** Easy to Moderate

## Step-by-Step Directions

### Segment 1: Oak Avenue (97 m / 0.06 mi)
- Start at 45 Oak Avenue, Boulder, CO 80304
- Head south on Oak Avenue
- Continue for approximately 3 blocks
- Duration: ~2 minutes

### Segment 2: 19th Street (2.16 km / 1.34 mi)
- Turn onto 19th Street (main southbound corridor)
- This is the longest segment -- enjoy the tree-lined ride
- Pass through the 13th Street area
- Continue past the University of Colorado campus area
- Duration: ~25-30 minutes at walking pace

### Segment 3: 20th Street (106 m / 0.07 mi)
- Brief jog east on 20th Street
- Connect from 19th to the next southbound street
- Duration: ~1 minute

### Segment 4: 20th Street South (566 m / 0.35 mi)
- Continue south on 20th Street
- Pass near the Pearl Street Mall western edge
- Duration: ~6-7 minutes

### Segment 5: Pearl Street (216 m / 0.13 mi)
- Cross onto Pearl Street -- historic heart of Boulder
- Passing through the famous Pearl Street Mall area
- Great spot for a coffee or water break
- Duration: ~2-3 minutes

### Segment 6: 18th Street (249 m / 0.15 mi)
- Continue south on 18th Street
- Short connector segment
- Duration: ~3 minutes

### Segment 7: Canyon Boulevard (637 m / 0.39 mi)
- Final stretch west on Canyon Boulevard
- Return Destination: 1800 Broadway, Boulder, CO 80302
- Duration: ~2 minutes

## What to Bring

- Water bottle -- High altitude hydration is critical!
- Sunscreen -- UV is stronger at 5,430 ft elevation
- Hat -- Protection from intense mountain sun
- Phone -- For navigation and emergencies
- Cash -- For Pearl Street coffee shops!

## Seasonal Considerations

- **Spring (Mar-May):** Beautiful wildflowers, occasional snow patches
- **Summer (Jun-Aug):** Warm days, afternoon thunderstorms common
- **Fall (Sep-Nov):** Crisp air, golden aspens along 19th Street
- **Winter (Dec-Feb):** Snow possible, dress in layers, route still plowed
