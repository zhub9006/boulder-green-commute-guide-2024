# Biking Route: 45 Oak Avenue to 1800 Broadway

## Route Summary

- **Distance:** 4.03 km (2.5 miles)
- **Elevation Gain:** ~150 ft (net descent)
- **Typical Duration:** 10-15 minutes
- **Optimal Duration:** ~8 minutes
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Bike Infrastructure:** Bike lanes on 19th Street, designated bike routes

## Step-by-Step Directions

### Segment 1: Oak Avenue (97 m / 0.06 mi)
- Start at 45 Oak Avenue, Boulder, CO 80304
- Head south on Oak Avenue
- Join 19th Street at the intersection
- Duration: ~30 seconds

### Segment 2: 19th Street -- Primary Bike Corridor (2.16 km / 1.34 mi)
- Main bike route -- dedicated bike lanes available
- This is Boulder's primary north-south bike corridor
- Ride through the residential and university areas
- Watch for traffic at cross streets (13th, 9th, 6th, etc.)
- Duration: ~4-6 minutes

### Segment 3: 20th Street Connector (106 m / 0.07 mi)
- Brief jog east to reconnect southbound
- Well-marked bike route
- Duration: ~15 seconds

### Segment 4: 20th Street South (566 m / 0.35 mi)
- Continue south on 20th Street
- Bike-compliant route with low traffic
- Duration: ~1.5 minutes

### Segment 5: Pearl Street (216 m / 0.13 mi)
- Cross onto Pearl Street
- Note: Pearl Street Mall is pedestrian-priority below 15th Street
- Use designated bike crossings
- Duration: ~45 seconds

### Segment 6: 18th Street (249 m / 0.15 mi)
- Quick southbound connector
- Duration: ~40 seconds

### Segment 7: Canyon Boulevard (637 m / 0.39 mi)
- Final stretch to 1800 Broadway
- Canyon Boulevard has designated bike infrastructure
- Duration: ~1.5 minutes

**Arrive at 1800 Broadway, Boulder, CO 80302**

## Bike Parking at Destination

- Bike racks available at 1800 Broadway office building
- Secure indoor parking available -- check with building management
- BCycle docking stations nearby

## Bike-Share Option: BCycle Boulder

- Station: Multiple stations along 19th Street and Pearl Street
- Cost: $12/month unlimited 60-min rides, or $1/30 min pay-as-you-go
- App: Download BCycle app for real-time station availability
- Tip: Check station availability before your ride!

## Safety Tips

- Wear a helmet -- Boulder law requires helmets for riders under 18
- Use your bell or voice at intersections
- Make eye contact with drivers at cross streets
- Use lights if commuting before 7 AM or after 7 PM
- Consider high-visibility clothing during rush hour
- Avoid headphones -- stay aware of traffic sounds

## Seasonal Notes

- Year-round commuting: Boulder maintains bike lanes through winter
- Spring: Watch for sand and grit on streets
- Summer: Afternoon thunderstorms -- plan accordingly
- Fall: Leaf-covered streets can be slippery
- Winter: Snow and ice -- use studded tires if commuting regularly
